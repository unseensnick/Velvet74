RP2040 Inner Column Module

Chip-down RP2040 controller for a Choc split keyboard, placed on the back of a 19.56 x 41.6 mm strip beside the inner key column, with the USB-C mouth on the top edge (Keebart Sofle Choc Pro style).

USB-C with ESD protection and a polyfuse, AP2112K 3.3 V regulator, W25Q128 16 MB flash, 12 MHz crystal, BOOT and RESET buttons. All 30 GPIOs are brought out as schematic labels. Parts carry LCSC numbers for JLCPCB assembly.

Parts are placed, not routed.
